Entertainment

Every property begins as one child’s question and grows only as far as the story can honestly go.

A series, then music, then a book, then a stage, then a room a family can walk into. The order is not commercial — it follows the story. We never build a franchise in order to sell products; we build products because they help a story keep living.

The rule that governs this world

No expansion is allowed to know the ending.

A game may not unlock an achievement announcing where a child’s curiosity leads before she has felt it herself. A book may not draw a future the story has not reached. Outside the fiction — a cover, a catalogue line — a real achievement may be named. Inside it, nobody knows.

Where it began

Present

On a stage, with a piano, in front of children.

The stage came first: ten years of didactic concerts in front of school audiences. The universe now being built is the continuation of that work, not a departure from it. Four children, four centuries, four crafts — each arriving with a question no other protagonist already carries.

The owned symbol

Present

La Chispa: curiosity, made visible.

She never speaks, never explains, never resolves. She appears at the birth of a genuine question, and then she leaves.
