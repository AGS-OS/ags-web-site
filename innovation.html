The rule that governs this world

No technology here may know a child’s future before the child does.

The law does not care which instrument breaks it. A narrator, a soundtrack, a camera move and a model are all capable of the same violation: signalling an ending the protagonist has not reached. Tools speed up the work. They never make the judgement.
