Culture

Culture is not decoration at the end of a budget. It is infrastructure.

It is how a territory decides what its children will believe is possible. That work is slow, unglamorous and mostly invisible while it happens — and it is where a generation is actually formed. We come to it from inside public institutions, not from a consultancy.

The rule that governs this world

A strategy that cannot outlive the administration that ordered it is a campaign, not a policy.

So the deliverable is never a document. It is a mechanism: who decides, on what evidence, with what budget, and what happens to the programme when the person who championed it leaves. If a plan needs its author in the room to survive, it is not finished.
