Governance

Who approves

Nothing enters production without passing the review. Final responsibility sits with creative direction — not with a committee, and not with commercial consensus.

Difficult cases

Where a historical figure’s legacy includes documented harm, inclusion requires an explicit, documented review before production. Never one writer, never under schedule pressure.

What cannot change

The law of shared discovery, and the creative laws that follow from it.

What can

Standards and operating criteria, against one test: does the change strengthen the law, or only make it more comfortable to comply with?

The rule that governs this world

Could this decision have been made by any other studio in the world?

If the answer is yes, it is not yet an AGS decision. That question survives every reorganisation of this house, and it is the one we apply to a script, a character, a piece of merchandising and a partnership alike.
